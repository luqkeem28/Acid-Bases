What this chapter is about

Chapter 3 of Organic Chemistry covers acids and bases, and it is the base for every reaction mechanism that comes after it. Two definitions run through it. In Brønsted–Lowry, an acid donates H⁺ and a base accepts it. In Lewis, an acid accepts an electron pair and a base donates one.

Then comes the useful part: use pKa to predict which way an equilibrium goes (it favours the weaker acid), and use four factors to explain why one acid beats another.
